## 🌧️ Premise & Atmosphere

It’s late autumn, 1948. Rain hammers against the frosted glass of the Homicide Bureau at Precinct 8. You are the Lead Investigator assigned to the Department’s unresolved homicide archives.

Rather than relying on simple dialogue trees, *Detective Stories* puts **forensic science and deduction** in your hands. Suspects lie, deflect, or feign innocence. To break their alibis, you must compare their statements against hard physical evidence, analyze autonomic vocal cord tremors on a phosphor oscilloscope, and catch fleeting 140ms micro-smirks before filing a formal indictment with the Grand Jury.

---

## 🔍 Key Investigative Systems

### 1. 48-Point Facial Landmark & Micro-Expression Mesh
- Visualizes real-time physiological facial stress overlaid directly onto vintage portrait photography.
- Tracks sub-second **unilateral micro-smirks** (a prime tell for narcissistic sociopaths feeling intellectually superior).
- Monitors autonomic **pupillary dilation vs. pinpoint constriction** ($mm$), lip compression ($0-100\%$), and saccadic glance aversion.

### 2. Spectral Voice Pattern & Acoustic Oscilloscope
- Simulates CRT oscilloscope frequency graphing with green/amber phosphor traces.
- Measures **pitch jitter ($Hz$)** and vocal cord micro-tremors. A sudden pitch jump of $+6.0\text{ Hz}$ or higher above baseline indicates conscious fabrication.
- Measures **speech latency pauses** before answers.

### 3. Procedural Pixel-Noir & Live Human Rigs
- Seamlessly toggle between:
  - **Pixel Noir Avatar**: 1940s chiaroscuro pixel art with volumetric cigarette smoke particle physics and chromatic aberration under deception.
  - **Live Human Avatar**: Landmark-anchored facial deformation with procedural mouth phoneme articulation and natural blinking saccades.

### 4. Conversational AI Interrogation Room
- **Natural Language Chat**: Ask questions in your own words using 1940s detective vernacular.
- **Push-to-Talk Microphone**: Click the microphone button to question suspects with your own voice. Speech is transcribed and answered in real time.
- **Curated Forensic Probes**: Ready-to-use tactical questions tailored to each suspect and case.
- **Vintage Voice Synthesis**: Spoken dialogue powered by character-specific acoustic modeling.

### 5. Procedural Web Audio Soundscape
- Synthesizes 1940s atmosphere without external audio downloads:
  - Procedural pink-noise **rain and tape hiss**.
  - **Cardiovascular pulse beeps** synced to suspect heart rates.
  - **Mechanical typewriter keystrokes** and radar pings.
  - Harmonic 4-chord celebration chimes upon breaking lies.

## 📁 The Cold Case Files

### Case 1: *The Velvet Ash Murder* (`#COLD-1948-0914`)
- **Victim**: Industrialist Arthur Sterling.
- **Crime Scene**: Locked 14th-floor penthouse library at 88 Blackwood Terrace. Smashed Patek Philippe watch stopped at 11:42 PM; crystal tumbler on desk.
- **Cause of Death**: Sudden cardiac arrhythmia induced by purified **aconitine** (wolfsbane extract) infused into 25-year Glenmorangie scotch.
- **Suspects**:
  - **Julian Vance**: Aristocratic art gallery director. Claims Sterling phoned him at 11:30 PM.
  - **Evelyn Cross**: Pianist and Sterling's ward. Naturally high anxiety baseline (94 bpm).
  - **Marcus Drake**: Discharged veteran and chauffeur. Stoic baseline.
  - **Dr. Aris Thorne**: Botanical chemist and dispensary toxicologist.

### Case 2: *The Pier 14 Blackout* (`#COLD-1948-1102`)
- **Victim**: Dockmaster Silas O’Malley.
- **Crime Scene**: Winch engine cradle at Berth 4, Pier 14 Slipway. Power breaker switch jammed with a 1-inch machinist spanner during a 01:00 AM blackout.
- **Cause of Death**: Acute respiratory paralysis caused by purified **$d$-tubocurarine** alkaloid spiked into O'Malley's steel coffee thermos.
- **Suspects**:
  - **Julian Vance**: Art dealer smuggling forged antiquities from Rotterdam.
  - **Marcus Drake**: Slipway diesel mechanic working in the upper machine loft.
  - **Dr. Aris Thorne**: Forensic toxicologist with a pharmacy dispensary on the wharf.

> **Note on Case Isolation**: Each case operates as a completely decoupled investigation. Characters appearing in both cases possess independent alibis, secrets, and motives with zero narrative cross-contamination.

## 🎮 Detective Field Guide: How to Play

### Phase 1: Review the Crime Dossier
1. Open the **Case Files** tab and select an active investigation.
2. Read the **Crime Scene Dossier** for entry points, locked doors, and timestamps.
3. Review the **Coroner's Toxicology Certificate** to note the exact poison, absorption rate, and time-of-death window.

### Phase 2: Inspect the Evidence Locker
1. Switch to the **Evidence Locker** tab.
2. Filter exhibits by *Documents*, *Forensics*, and *Physical Items*.
3. Note discrepancies between forensic exhibits (e.g., severed phone cables, missing keys, timestamps on receipts).

### Phase 3: Calibrate the Psychological Baseline
1. Open the **Psych Profiles** tab.
2. Run baseline calibration questions to discover each suspect's normal resting vitals (heart rate, pitch jitter, glance aversion).
3. Read the **False-Positive Advisory** to prevent wrongful accusations.

### Phase 4: Interrogate the Suspects
1. Enter the **Interrogation Suite**.
2. Select a suspect from the roster.
3. Ask questions via **Text**, **Microphone**, or **Curated Forensic Probes**.
4. Observe their vitals HUD:
   - Is their heart rate spiking abnormally high above their personal baseline?
   - Is their pitch jitter exceeding $+6.0\text{ Hz}$?
   - Did their mouth twitch into an asymmetric micro-smirk?
5. When you catch a suspect in a lie, confront them with the contradictory exhibit. A successful confrontation **cracks** the statement and forces an admission!

### Phase 5: File the Grand Jury Indictment
1. When you have broken the suspect's alibi and assembled your theory, click **Indict** in the top navigation bar.
2. Select:
   - **The Prime Accused**
   - **The Murder Weapon / Execution Method**
   - **The Decisive Contradicting Exhibit**
3. Submit the indictment to the District Attorney. A correct theory earns a 1000-point conviction, awarding the rank of **Chief Superintendent of Cold Cases** and archiving the case as **SOLVED**.

## 🛡️ The False-Positive Guard (Detective Shield)

A central pillar of the game is distinguishing **natural anxiety or cynical stoicism** from **genuine guilt**:

| Suspect Archetype | Natural Baseline | Common Pitfall / False Positive | True Deception Tell |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Type-B Neurotic Avoidant**<br>*(Evelyn Cross)* | 92–96 BPM pulse, 28% tremor, voice quaver. | **Do NOT accuse based on anxiety!** Her vitals are elevated even when telling the absolute truth. | Contradictions with financial records or physical whereabouts. |
| **Machiavellian Sociopath**<br>*(Julian Vance)* | 58 BPM resting pulse, 0% glance aversion, ice-cold composure. | **Composure does NOT equal innocence!** He shows no outward panic while lying. | 140ms unilateral micro-smirks and physical timeline contradictions. |
| **Introverted Cynical Stoic**<br>*(Marcus Drake)* | 70 BPM pulse, 48–50% eye contact aversion, terse delivery. | **Looking away is his defense mechanism against badges**, not proof of murder. | Inability to explain whereabouts during power cuts or physical evidence tampering. |
| **Obsessive-Compulsive**<br>*(Dr. Aris Thorne)* | Rapid clipped diction (0.3s latency), intellectual disdain. | **Haughty arrogance is part of his baseline personality.** | Sudden speech latency pauses ($>2.0\text{s}$) and pinpoint pupillary constriction ($<2.5\text{ mm}$). |

### Hardboiled Mode
In the **Options** menu, you can toggle the **Detective Shield OFF**:
- Removes protective warnings and redacts psychological cheat-sheets.
- Tests your raw deductive skill—one false accusation will blow your case!

## 💻 Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Lucide Icons, HTML5 Canvas.
- **Backend**: Express on Node.js, `tsx` runner, `@google/genai` TypeScript SDK.
- **Audio Synthesizer**: Procedural Web Audio API (oscillators, biquad filters, pink-noise generators).
- **Speech Recognition**: Gemini Multimodal Audio transcription (`/api/transcribe`).
- **Text-to-Speech**: Gemini Studio TTS (`gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`) via `/api/speak` with browser SpeechSynthesis fallback.

## 🚀 Installation & Local Setup

### Prerequisites
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) v18.0.0 or higher
- npm (bundled with Node) or [Bun](https://bun.sh/)

### 1. Clone the Repository
```bash
git clone https://github.com/your-username/detective-stories.git
cd detective-stories
```

### 2. Install Dependencies
```bash
npm install
```

### 3. Configure Environment Variables
Copy the example environment configuration:
```bash
cp .env.example .env
```
Open `.env` and add your Google Gemini API key:
```env
GEMINI_API_KEY="your-gemini-api-key-here"
PORT=3000
```
*(Note: If no API key is provided, the game automatically runs with high-quality deterministic noir fallback dialogue and full client-side Web Audio/Speech synthesis).*

### 4. Run the Full-Stack Development Server
```bash
npm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

### 5. Build for Production
```bash
npm run build
npm start
```

## 🕹️ Publishing to Itch.io (HTML5)

The game is designed to run seamlessly as an **HTML5 browser game** on [itch.io](https://itch.io).

### Option 1: Standalone HTML5 Game (Easiest)
1. Run `npm run build`.
2. In the game UI, open **Options** and click **Download .ZIP** (or download `/download-game-zip` directly).
3. On itch.io, create a new project:
   - **Kind of project**: Select **HTML**.
   - **Uploads**: Upload `detective-stories-itch.zip` and check **☑ This file will be played in the browser**.
   - **Embed Options**: Set viewport to `1280` × `720` (or `1280` × `800`), check **Fullscreen button** and **Automatically start on page load**.
4. Set status to **Public** and publish!

### Option 2: Full-Stack on Itch.io + Hosted AI Backend
1. Host `server.ts` on any cloud container (Cloud Run, Render, Railway, Fly.io).
2. Set `VITE_API_BASE_URL="https://your-backend-service.com"` in your environment before building.
3. The Itch.io browser client will automatically communicate with your backend via CORS.

## ⚙️ Configuration & Telemetry

All configurable parameters are centralized in `src/config.ts`:
- **AI Models**: Primary chat (`gemini-3.8-flash`), TTS (`gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts`).
- **Biometric Calibration**: Pulse bounds (58–105 bpm), pitch jitter deception threshold ($6.0\text{ Hz}$), pupil constriction/dilation thresholds ($2.5-4.2\text{ mm}$).
- **Audio Frequencies**: Typewriter frequencies ($800\text{ Hz}$), rain low-pass filter ($600\text{ Hz}$), buzzer notes ($110\text{ Hz}$).
- **Sanitized Telemetry**: All function invocations and GenAI calls log to `console.info` with base64 and binary attachments automatically stripped via `src/utils/logger.ts`.
